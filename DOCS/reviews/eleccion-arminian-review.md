# Adversarial review — ELECCIÓN, Sección 2 (arminiana y wesleyana)

## Verdict

**SOUND.** The section is a faithful, careful presentation of _classical_ Arminianism
in its own terms, following the Five Articles of the Remonstrance (1610) in order.
Total inability is genuinely affirmed (not softened); prevenient grace is correctly
presented as grace that precedes and enables, monergistic in its initiation; there is
no semi-Pelagian drift and no strawman. Every Bible reference resolves in
`bible-seed.db` (RVR1960) and every quoted fragment is verbatim **except one**
(1 P 1:5, a one-word deviation inside quotation marks). No cross-characterization of
the Reformed view anywhere in the section. The Arminius / Wesley / 1618 material is
correctly attributed and clearly distinguished from what 1610 itself said.

One SHOULD-FIX and five NICE-TO-HAVE findings below. Nothing blocks.

## 1. Faithfulness to classical Arminianism — PASS

- **Art. 3 / total inability.** Line 61 renders Art. III in its strongest form: man "no
  puede por sí mismo pensar, querer ni hacer nada verdaderamente bueno", and the draft
  adds "Esto incluye la fe: nadie puede creer para salvación por sus propias fuerzas."
  This is **not** a softened version. It faithfully tracks Art. III's own parenthetical
  "(such as saving Faith eminently is)", confirmed against holyjoys.org and
  evangelicalarminians.org reproductions of the 1610 text. Art. III also cites John 15:5
  "Without me ye can do nothing"; the draft's prooftext at line 65 ("separados de mí
  nada podéis hacer", Jn 15:5) is therefore the Article's _own_ prooftext — a strength.
- **Arminius, Disputation XI** (line 63). Verbatim English (The Works of Arminius,
  "Twenty-Five Public Disputations", XI, "On the Free Will of Man and its Powers"):
  _"…the Free Will of man towards the True Good is not only wounded, maimed, infirm,
  bent, and weakened; but it is also imprisoned, destroyed, and lost: And its powers are
  not only debilitated and useless unless they be assisted by grace, but it has no
  powers whatever except such as are excited by Divine grace."_
  The draft's "no solo está herido y debilitado, sino preso, destruido y perdido, y que
  no le queda poder alguno salvo el que la gracia divina despierta en él" is a faithful
  rendering. The list "wounded, maimed, infirm, bent, and weakened" is compressed to
  "herido y debilitado" — acceptable because it is presented as a paraphrase, not a
  quotation, and the compression does not weaken the sense. **Not in quotation marks —
  correct.**
- **Prevenient grace, monergistic in initiation.** Line 51: "La fe que Dios prevé es
  ella misma obra de su gracia." Line 65: "La salvación empieza con un acto creador de
  Dios." Line 71: "Antes de que nadie la busque, Dios ya obra." Line 75: "El creyente
  obra porque Dios obra primero en él." The initiation is consistently God's; the human
  never "contributes the decisive bit." No semi-Pelagianism.
- **"colaboradores suyos" (line 75).** Reasonable (echoes 1 Co 3:9) and guarded by the
  immediately preceding "Dios es el que en vosotros produce así el querer como el
  hacer". Synergism _in the response of the regenerate_, which is classical Arminian.
  Not a finding.
- **Would an Arminian reader say "that's not what we hold"?** No. The conditional-
  election / universal-atonement / resistible-grace / conditional-perseverance package
  is the mainstream classical position. The perseverance handling (see §2 below) is
  accurate about the 1610 → 1618 development.
- **Would a Reformed reader fairly cry "strawman"?** No. If anything the section states
  the _most robust_ form of Arminianism (grace alone, total inability). That is the
  design intent.

## 2. Confessional accuracy — PASS (one wording note, finding #2)

Each of the Five Articles was checked against the standard English text retrieved this
session (Wikipedia "Five Articles of Remonstrance" for full Art. V; ccel.org Schaff
_Creeds of Christendom_ vol. III / creeds3.iv.xv.html for Arts. IV–V; holyjoys.org for
Arts. III–IV detail; crivoice.org and evangelicalarminians.org for the I–V summary).

- **Art. 1** (line 49) — "propósito eterno e inmutable en Jesucristo su Hijo… antes de
  la fundación del mundo, salvar en Cristo y por causa de Cristo a quienes, movidos por
  la gracia del Espíritu Santo, crean… y perseveren… hasta el fin." Matches
  ("an eternal, unchangeable purpose in Jesus Christ his Son, before the foundation of
  the world… to save in Christ, for Christ's sake… those who, through the grace of the
  Holy Ghost, shall believe… and shall persevere… even to the end"). The draft omits
  Art. 1's second half (God's decree "to leave the incorrigible and unbelieving in sin
  and under wrath, and to condemn them"). One-sided but not misrepresentation; the
  section is explicitly scoped to election. See finding #2-adjacent note.
- **Arminius's "cuarto decreto"** (line 51), cited to the _Declaración de sentimientos_
  (Declaration of Sentiments, 1608). Arminius's fourth decree: God "decreed to save and
  damn certain particular persons," founded on foreknowledge of "those individuals who
  would, through his preceding grace, believe, and, through his subsequent grace, would
  persevere." The draft's "decretó salvar a las personas concretas que, según su
  presciencia, movidas por su gracia, creerían y perseverarían" is faithful (again the
  damnation half is dropped; same scoping note).
- **Art. 2** (line 55) — "murió por todos los hombres y por cada hombre… obtuvo para
  todos ellos la redención y el perdón de los pecados; pero nadie disfruta de hecho ese
  perdón sino el creyente." Verbatim-faithful to Art. II ("died for all men and for
  every man, so that he has obtained for them all, by his death on the cross,
  redemption and the forgiveness of sins; yet that no one actually enjoys this
  forgiveness of sins except the believer").
- **Art. 3** (line 61) — faithful; see §1 above.
- **Art. 4** (line 69) — "la gracia es el principio, la continuación y el cumplimiento
  de todo bien, de modo que ni siquiera el regenerado obra el bien sin ella… no es
  irresistible, pues está escrito de muchos que resistieron al Espíritu Santo." Matches
  Art. IV ("the beginning, continuance, and accomplishment of all good… the regenerate
  man himself, without prevenient or assisting, awakening, following and cooperative
  grace, can neither think, will, nor do good… as respects the mode of the operation of
  this grace, it is not irresistible; inasmuch as it is written concerning many, that
  they have resisted the Holy Ghost"). **"no es irresistible" is not in quotation marks
  — correct.** See finding #2 for the dropped adjective "prevenient".
- **Art. 5** (lines 79, 81) — opening ("pleno poder para luchar contra Satanás, el
  pecado, el mundo y su propia carne… Cristo mismo los guarda de caer") matches Art. V's
  opening. The perseverance-left-open sentence (line 81) is **accurate**: "Sobre una
  sola cuestión los artículos de 1610 se declararon aún indecisos: si un creyente
  verdadero podría, por negligencia, abandonar el principio de su vida en Cristo y
  perder la gracia. Eso… debía determinarse con más cuidado a partir de la Sagrada
  Escritura antes de enseñarlo con plena convicción (art. 5)." This tracks Art. V's
  close ("whether they are capable, through negligence, of forsaking again the first
  beginning of their life in Christ… of becoming devoid of grace, that must be more
  particularly determined out of the Holy Scripture, before we ourselves can teach it
  with the full persuasion of our minds"). See finding #5 for the omitted conditional.
- **"Later resolution" — correctly attributed and distinguished** (line 83). The draft
  clearly separates 1610 ("se declararon aún indecisos") from the later position ("Al
  estudiarlo más, la teología arminiana y wesleyana posterior respondió que sí"),
  attributing the affirmative answer to (a) the 1618 _Opinions of the Remonstrants_
  ("los creyentes verdaderos pueden, por su propia culpa, caer finalmente y perecer")
  and (b) Wesley. Both are correct. The 1618 _Sententiae Remonstrantium_, 5th point:
  "true believers are able to fall through their own fault into shameful and atrocious
  deeds… and therefore finally to fall and to perish." Wesley affirmed final apostasy
  of a true believer (e.g. "Serious Thoughts upon the Perseverance of the Saints",
  1751; sermon "A Call to Backsliders"). **None of this material is quoted — correct.**
- **Wesley, prevenient grace** (line 71) — "nadie peca por no tener gracia, sino por no
  usar la gracia que ya tiene", cited to the sermon "Sobre ocuparnos en nuestra propia
  salvación." Confirmed: Sermon 85, "On Working Out Our Own Salvation" (text: Phil
  2:12-13), §III.4: "no man sins because he has not grace, but because he does not use
  the grace which he hath." Paraphrased, not quoted — correct. Good internal
  consistency: the sermon's text is Phil 2:12-13, which the draft quotes at line 75.

## 6. Cross-characterization of the Reformed view — CLEAN

No sentence in Section 2 describes, contrasts with, or rebuts the Reformed position.
Checked specifically:

- "no es irresistible" (line 69) is Article IV's own self-description of the _mode_ of
  grace; it names no opposing system. Representing Art. IV faithfully requires it.
- "puede ser rechazada" / "puede ser resistida" (line 73) — same: exposition of the
  Remonstrant view, not a critique of anyone.
- The closing lines (85, 87) speak only of "esta tradición" and its own gospel logic.

All contrast work is left to the orchestrator (Sections 1 and 4), as designed.

## 7. Length / balance vs Section 3 — BALANCED (one asymmetry, finding #3)

- **Length:** §2 ≈ 7,422 chars, §3 ≈ 7,676 (per the draft's own headers). ~3% apart —
  not an imbalance.
- **Structure:** both are 5-part, each part = confession-paraphrase + cited primary
  source + woven RVR prooftexts; both open with a grace-affirming frame and close with
  a doxological "toda la gloria / alabanza… de Dios" line. Symmetric.
- **Source spread:** §3 names Dort + Westminster + Calvin's _Institución_; §2 names the

- **Contested prooftexts stated declaratively:** both sides do this symmetrically — §2

- **Asymmetry (finding #3):** §3 defuses its _own_ caricature labels inline, twice —
  "depravación total… no afirma que cada persona sea tan mala como podría ser" and

## 8. Theological overreach — no BLOCKER; see findings #3, #4

The section is generally careful to frame claims as the tradition's exposition
("La Escritura llama a los creyentes…", "El cuarto artículo confiesa…", "Arminio lo
llamó…"). Two spots where a bare-text conclusion is asserted more strongly than the
text itself supports:

- **Heb 6:4-6 (line 83)** — finding #4. "es posible haber sido iluminado y hecho
  partícipe del Espíritu Santo y, aun así, recaer" takes the passage's _premise_ and

  arrepentimiento"). Also sits awkwardly against §2's own pastoral close (line 85).

  inside the Article-2 exposition and §3 does the mirror-image thing with its own
  distinctives ("compra para ellos la fe misma"). Symmetric — not flagged as a separate
  finding, noted for the orchestrator's awareness.

# FINDINGS (detail + minimal fixes)

## Finding 1 — SHOULD-FIX — 1 P 1:5 quoted non-verbatim

**Location:** line 79.
**Text:** `y el creyente es "guardado por el poder de Dios mediante la fe" (1 P 1:5).`
**Problem:** RVR1960 1 Pedro 1:5 reads **"que sois guardados por el poder de Dios
mediante la fe"** — plural _guardados_. The draft alters it to singular _guardado_ to
agree with "el creyente" and keeps the quotation marks. Under the section's stated
quote-mark discipline (quotation marks = verbatim RVR1960 only), altered text may not
sit inside quotes.
**Why SHOULD-FIX, not NICE-TO-HAVE:** this is an in-repo consistency defect, not
pedantry. (a) Section 3 cites the _same verse_ at line 115 unquoted:
"somos guardados por el poder de Dios mediante la fe (1 P 1:5)". (b) The draft's own
status note (line 17) records the **identical** fix already applied to 2 P 1:10 in
Section 4 — quotes removed because RVR's "vuestra" clashed with third-person prose.
Section 2 has the same problem unfixed.
**Minimal fix:** drop the quotation marks (match §3 / the line-17 precedent exactly):

> y el creyente es guardado por el poder de Dios mediante la fe (1 P 1:5).

## Finding 2 — NICE-TO-HAVE — Art. 4 paraphrase drops "preveniente"

**Location:** line 69 (paraphrase of Article IV); heading at line 67 is
"**Cuarto artículo: la gracia preveniente y resistible**".
**Problem:** Article IV's own text names the grace "**prevenient** or assisting,
awakening, following and cooperative" — confirmed against holyjoys.org and
evangelicalarminians.org. The draft compresses this to "ni siquiera el regenerado obra
el bien sin ella" and drops "prevenient". The word then reappears at line 71 only as
the _author's_ gloss ("es _preveniente_ porque precede"), supported by Jn 1:9 / Jn
12:32 rather than by the Article. The section heading's key technical term is thus
grounded in inference, not in the 1610 document — when the document supplies it
directly.
**Minimal fix:** restore the adjective chain into the Article-4 paraphrase, e.g.:

> …de modo que ni siquiera el regenerado obra el bien sin la gracia que previene,
> asiste, despierta, acompaña y coopera; toda buena obra se debe a la gracia de Dios
> en Cristo.

This improves confessional accuracy and anchors the heading term in Art. 4.
(Adjacent, optional: Art. 1 and Arminius's 4th decree paraphrases both silently drop
the reprobation/condemnation half of their sources. Defensible given the section's
scope, but a half-clause — "y dejar bajo condenación a los incrédulos" — would make
the paraphrases complete.)

## Finding 3 — NICE-TO-HAVE — §2 never defuses its own caricature inline

**Location:** after "no es irresistible" (line 69) or in the line 75 paragraph.
**Problem:** §3 twice pre-empts its own hostile labels inline (depravity ≠ "as bad as
possible"; efficacious ≠ "forced"). §2 never does the parallel for the Arminian side —
the "Arminianism = man saves himself" caricature is answered only in §4. An inline
clause would restore the symmetry and is design-compliant: **self-description is not
contrast**, so it does not intrude on the orchestrator's job.
**Minimal fix:** one self-descriptive clause, e.g. after line 69:

> Que la gracia pueda resistirse no significa que la persona aporte algo de su parte a
> la salvación: aun el poder de responder es don de esa misma gracia.

(No mention of, or comparison with, the Reformed view — pure self-description.)

## Finding 4 — NICE-TO-HAVE — Heb 6:4-6 premise kept, conclusion dropped

**Location:** line 83.
**Text:** `es posible haber sido iluminado y hecho partícipe del Espíritu Santo y, aun
así, recaer (Heb 6:4-6)`.
**Problem:** the passage's salient word is **"es imposible… sean otra vez renovados
para arrepentimiento"**. The draft uses the passage to establish "es posible… recaer"
and is silent on the irremediability the passage actually asserts — which also sits
awkwardly against §2's own next line ("la promesa de vida es firme para todo el que se
mantiene en Cristo por la fe").
**Minimal fix:** frame as the writer's warning rather than a bare possibility claim:

> el autor de Hebreos advierte a quienes fueron iluminados y hechos partícipes del
> Espíritu Santo contra recaer (Heb 6:4-6);

## Finding 5 — NICE-TO-HAVE — Art. 5 conditional omitted

**Location:** line 79.
**Text:** `Cristo mismo los guarda de caer (Remonstrancia de 1610, art. 5)`.
**Problem:** Article V's "keeps them from falling" is explicitly conditioned. Verbatim
(retrieved from en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Articles_of_Remonstrance and confirmed
against ccel.org Schaff, _Creeds of Christendom_ vol. III, creeds3.iv.xv.html):
"…and that Jesus Christ assists them through his Spirit in all temptations, extends to
them his hand, and **if only they are ready for the conflict, and desire his help, and
are not inactive**, keeps them from falling…". Dropping the condition makes the 1610
view look slightly more monergistic-on-perseverance than it is. (Low weight: the
section then develops the falling-away possibility at length, so the net picture is not
misleading.)
**Minimal fix:** add the condition:

> …y, con tal que el creyente no se descuide y busque su ayuda, Cristo mismo lo guarda
> de caer (Remonstrancia de 1610, art. 5).

## Finding 6 — NICE-TO-HAVE — three small polish items

- **"(_Disputación XI_)" (line 63).** It is one of Arminius's _Public Disputations_, not
  a standalone work. Consider "(Disputaciones públicas, XI: «Sobre el libre albedrío del
  hombre»)" for precision.
- **"Cristo, levantado en la cruz, declara" (line 71).** RVR Jn 12:32 reads "si fuere
  levantado **de la tierra**"; the quoted fragment ("a todos atraeré a mí mismo") is
  verbatim and Jn 12:33 supports the cross gloss, so this is defensible — but
  "levantado de la tierra" would match the text the reader sees.
- **Jn 15:6 paraphrase "es cortado y quemado" (line 83).** RVR describes the branch as
  "echado fuera… se secará… los echan en el fuego, y arden" (withered, then burned) —
  no cutting. "cortado" imports Jn 15:2. Consider "es echado fuera y quemado".
