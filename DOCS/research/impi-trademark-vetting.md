# Vetting the "IMPI Aguascalientes" trademark message

Research date: 2026-08-31. Read-only desk research (web sources only). Nothing was
submitted, no form filled, no one contacted.

---

## VERDICT (bottom line)

**The message contains no fraud mechanism, but it is not a reliable source either, and
Victor should not act on it as written.** The contact appears to have copied information
from
`impi-gob.com.mx`, a lookalike domain that openly labels itself an _unofficial_
information portal ("PORTAL INFORMATIVO NO OFICIAL" — it does not collect data, take
payments, or file anything). The message contains **none** of the active fraud
mechanisms (no invoice, no "pay into this account", no urgency, no request for personal
or card data, no offer to file on his behalf). Most of its concrete facts are actually
**genuine but imprecisely labelled**: the Aguascalientes address is the real
**Secretaría de Economía "Oficina de Representación"** (which hosts an IMPI intake
desk — IMPI has no standalone office in Aguascalientes), the phone `55 5729 9100` is the
real Secretaría de Economía switchboard, and `@economia.gob.mx` is the correct email
domain for IMPI staff posted in those offices. Two things in the message are wrong or
stale: the fee (`$2,457.79` is a superseded amount; the current Tarifa figure for a
national trademark application is **$2,695.18 + IVA = $3,126.41 per class**), and the
named contact "Amanda Mirella Santoyo Reyes" could not be verified (the 2022 IMPI
directory lists a different person for that office). **Correct move:** ignore the URL and the named contact, and go
directly through `www.gob.mx/impi` and IMPI's own e-services
(`eservicios.impi.gob.mx` — "Tu cuenta PASE" / Marca en Línea). An individual can file
entirely online, on his own behalf, with no lawyer and no third party; he generates his
own payment line in PASE, so whatever number a blog quotes doesn't matter operationally.

## VERIFIED

### 1. The real official IMPI presence

- **Main site:** `www.gob.mx/impi` (federal government sites are `.gob.mx`).
- **E-services / filing:** `https://eservicios.impi.gob.mx/seimpi/` — "Tu cuenta PASE"
  (free account) is where you create and submit a trademark application ("Marca en
  Línea") and **generate your own payment line**. IMPI states plainly:
  _"TODOS LOS PAGOS SE GENERAN DIRECTAMENTE POR EL USUARIO, únicamente en el portal Tu
  cuenta PASE."_
- **Trademark search (free):** `marcanet.impi.gob.mx` / `acervomarcas.impi.gob.mx`
  (phonetic / denominación search before filing).
- **Appointments:** `citas.impi.gob.mx`. **Official mailbox:** `buzon@impi.gob.mx`.
- **IMPI central phone:** `55 5624 0400` (HQ: Periférico Sur 3106, Jardines del
  Pedregal, CDMX). This is _different_ from the `55 5729 9100` in the message (that one
  is the parent ministry's switchboard).
- IMPI now issues **digital** registration certificates (since 2025).

### 2. `impi-gob.com.mx` is NOT an official government domain

- It is a `.com.mx` domain built to look like `gob.mx/impi`. Its own footer says:
  _"impi-gob.com.mx no es una web oficial de esta entidad mexicana. Somos una comunidad
  que tiene como objetivo ofrecer información actualizada… No solicitamos datos
  personales. No realizamos trámites y/o gestiones de cualquier tipo relacionadas con
  IMPI."_ Copyright line: _"2026 © PORTAL INFORMATIVO NO OFICIAL."_
- Behaviourally it matches the well-known family of independent Mexican
  "trámites" info sites (e.g. `gobdemexico.com`, `gobierno.org`) that publish
  guidance, disclaim official status, and do not charge or process anything. On the
  pages reviewed it showed **no** payment capture, **no** lead form, **no** "we'll
  register it for you" offer, and no visible ads.
- **It is not the domain in the December 2025 IMPI/FGR fraud complaint** — that
  complaint names `registrodemarcasimpi.com`, a different site that used IMPI emblems
  and fake forms for phishing. Do not conflate the two.
- WHOIS / registrant / creation date for `impi-gob.com.mx` could **not** be retrieved
  (the `.mx` registry's public WHOIS is gated; third-party lookups returned nothing
  usable). Treated as unknown.

### 3. The fee

- Governing instrument: **"Acuerdo por el que se da a conocer la Tarifa por los
  servicios que presta el IMPI"**, DOF 23-08-1995, consolidated text, _última reforma
  DOF 15-03-2024_.
- **Article 14 a** (study of a national application for a trademark / aviso / nombre
  comercial through conclusion of the procedure — and since each application covers one
  class, this is effectively the per-class fee):
  **$2,695.18 MXN + 16% IVA = $3,126.41 MXN.**
  (Corroborated by multiple current 2025–2026 sources and internally consistent:
  2,695.18 × 1.16 = 3,126.41. The exact figure sits in the tariff table of the official
  Acuerdo; PDF text-extraction scrambled that column, so it is cited here as
  primary-instrument + multi-source corroborated rather than a clean primary quote.)
- **10% online discount:** Regla NOVENA of the Acuerdo — when art. 14a is filed through
  "Sistema Marca en Línea", a 10% discount applies → ≈ **$2,425.66 + IVA ≈ $2,813.77
  per class.**
- **The `$2,457.79` in the message** is a superseded amount (it was the art. 14a fee
  under an earlier version of the Tarifa; the exact date it changed was not confirmed
  in this research). It is _not_ the online-discounted price either
  (2,695.18 × 0.9 = 2,425.66 ≠ 2,457.79) — it is simply out of date. It is still shown
  on `impi-gob.com.mx` and even on some `economia.gob.mx` help pages
  (e.g. the VUMIPYMES "Obtención de registro de marca" guide), so quoting it is a
  common error, not a scam tell.
- **One application = one class. Mexico does NOT allow multi-class applications.**
  Protecting the brand in 3 classes = 3 separate applications, each with its own full
  fee and its own examination. (Confirmed by multiple IP-firm country guides; consistent
  with the Tarifa itself — art. 14c "renovación" says _"por cada clase"_, art. 14a does
  not, i.e. 14a already presupposes a single class.)
- **No indie/small-entity discount applies to trademarks.** The Tarifa's 50% reduction
  (Regla CUARTA) covers only patents, utility models, industrial designs and
  information services (arts. 1a–1f, 1i, 2–5, 9–13, 19–23, 26 BIS) — **article 14
  (marcas) is excluded.**
- **"Marcas para el Bienestar"** (a real ~90% discount → pay ~$312 total) is a
  **SEDECO Ciudad de México + IMPI** campaign, restricted to **Mexico City residents**
  in priority groups (artisans, micro-entrepreneurs, seniors, people with disabilities,
  youth, indigenous/Afro-descendant communities, co-op members), open only in short
  annual windows. Almost certainly not applicable to Victor, but worth knowing it
  exists if he is a CDMX resident in one of those groups.

### 4. Aguascalientes office

- IMPI's own regional-office directory (`Sistema_Citas_OR_Carta.pdf`, extracted) lists
  exactly **five** Oficinas Regionales plus the CDMX central offices — **none in
  Aguascalientes**:
  - **ORO — Occidente:** Zapopan, Jalisco (Blvd. Puerta de Hierro)
  - **ORN — Norte:** Monterrey, Nuevo León (Av. Fundidora, Edif. Cintermex)
  - **ORS — Sureste:** Mérida, Yucatán
  - **ORB — Bajío:** León, Guanajuato (Av. Paseo del Moral, Jardines del Moral) — this
    is the regional office whose territory covers **Aguascalientes, Guanajuato, San Luis
    Potosí and Zacatecas.**
  - **ORC — Centro:** San Andrés Cholula, Puebla
  - Central offices: Periférico Sur 3106, Jardines del Pedregal, CDMX (+ Xochimilco).
- That same directory says citizens book appointments and "se presentan en la
  **representación de la Secretaría de Economía**" — i.e. in states without a regional
  office, IMPI attends the public through the **Secretaría de Economía "Oficina de
  Representación"**. The one in Aguascalientes is exactly the address in the message
  (Av. Tecnológico 106, Ex-Hacienda Ojo Caliente, Col. 4° Centenario, C.P. 20190).
  IMPI's 2022 directory of _encargados_ lists the desk there under **Gloria Angélica
  Romo Valadéz, `gloria.romo@economia.gob.mx`, ext. 80210, tel. 449 689 0111.**
- So a physical intake point for Aguascalientes exists — but it is an SE representation
  office with an IMPI desk, not an "IMPI branch/sucursal", and the current staffer /
  phone / extension should be confirmed against IMPI's live directory, not this message.
  (Filing online via PASE makes the whole question moot.)

### 5. Scam-pattern check

Known Mexican IP-registration scam patterns (per IMPI's own "No te dejes sorprender"
notice, the Dec-2025 IMPI/FGR complaint, TV Azteca / Animal Político / Vanguardia
coverage, and the Lexology summary of WIPO/EUIPO "misleading invoices" warnings):

| Pattern                                                              | Present in this message?                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unsolicited postal letter/email demanding payment for "renewal"      | No                                                                                                      |
| Fake invoice / payment line / "capture line"                         | No                                                                                                      |
| Instruction to deposit into a **private / third-party bank account** | No                                                                                                      |
| Artificial urgency ("your mark will lapse / be cancelled")           | No                                                                                                      |
| Request for personal, ID or card data                                | No                                                                                                      |
| Offer by a third party to "do the trámite for you" for a markup      | No                                                                                                      |
| Lookalike domain instead of `gob.mx`                                 | **Yes** — `impi-gob.com.mx`                                                                             |
| A named "official" with a generic government email as the funnel     | **Partly** — a name is given, but with a correct `@economia.gob.mx` domain, and no payment ask attached |

IMPI's standing guidance, which this vets against:

- Its **only** payment channel is the user-generated line inside PASE
  (`eservicios.impi.gob.mx`).
- IMPI **never** sends letters/emails with payment formats or capture lines.
- IMPI **does not authorise** any company or person to do trámites in its name and
  **does not certify** third-party "specialists".
- Verify the site ends in `.gob.mx`.

Net: this message trips **1.5 of 8** patterns and none of the money-movement ones. It
reads as second-hand guidance sourced from an SEO info-site, not as an attack. The
residual risk is the lookalike domain + an unverifiable named contact — enough to say
"don't use it", not enough to call it fraud.

## The correct, safe way for an individual to file a Mexican trademark

1. **Create a free "Tu cuenta PASE"** at `https://eservicios.impi.gob.mx/seimpi/`.
   (~10 min.)
2. **Clearance search** (free) at `marcanet.impi.gob.mx` — phonetic + denominación
   search in the class(es) you'll use, and check the app name isn't descriptive /
   generic / religiously non-distinctive in a way that gets it refused.
3. **File "Solicitud de registro de marca" via Marca en Línea** inside PASE.
   - Applicant role: **solicitante persona física** (an individual can file **on their
     own behalf** — no lawyer, no _gestor_, no obligatory agent).
   - The application must state name, nationality, domicile, and **an address to
     receive notifications in Mexican territory** (_domicilio para oír y recibir
     notificaciones en territorio nacional_).
     - If Victor **resides in Mexico**: he uses his own Mexican address; done.
     - If Victor **resides abroad** (e.g. Spain): he still can be the applicant, but he
       needs a Mexican notification address — in practice most non-residents appoint a
       Mexican _apoderado_ / IP agent for this. (This is a domicile-for-service
       requirement, not a requirement to hand the case to anyone.)
   - **One application per class** — no multi-class filings in Mexico. Pick the class
     that matters most first (see below).
   - Mexico is **first-to-file** (priority goes to whoever files first, not whoever used
     it first), and — for registrations filed on/after 10 Aug 2018 — a
     **declaration of use** must be filed within 3 months after the 3rd anniversary of
     the _grant_, and again at each renewal; missing it cancels the registration.
     (Per Mexican IP-firm guidance; worth reconfirming the exact deadlines with IMPI or
     a Mexican IP attorney before relying on them.)
4. **Pay the government fee**, per class, on the **line PASE generates for you** —
   currently **$2,695.18 + IVA ($3,126.41)** per class, or ~**$2,813.77** per class
   with the 10% Marca en Línea discount. Card/SPEI/bank — always against IMPI's own
   payment line, never a personal account.
5. Registration term: **10 years**, renewable for equal periods.

### Nice classes for a Bible app (publishing / mobile software)

Pick the **minimum** set — each class is a full separate fee:

- **Class 9** — downloadable mobile application / downloadable software. This is the
  primary class for most Bible apps (the app as a product the user downloads).
- **Class 42** — SaaS, "software as a service", hosting / non-downloadable software.
  Add **only if** the app delivers core features from a server (accounts, sync,
  cloud content, AI features).
- **Class 41** — education, religious/spiritual instruction, providing online
  **non-downloadable** publications, electronic publishing. Add **only if** the brand
  will also be used for content/teaching services, not just the app.

Recommended starting point: **Class 9 alone**, unless there's a concrete reason to add
42 (server-delivered) or 41 (publishing/teaching services). He can always file
additional classes later.
